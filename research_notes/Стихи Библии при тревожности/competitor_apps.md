# Competitor Apps: Christian/Bible-Based Apps for Anxiety and Mental Wellbeing (as of Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-08. Many price/rating figures come from third-party trackers (App Pricing Lab, AppFollow, Sensor Tower, Appfigures) that snapshot at different dates. Older data is marked [OLDER: year]. Note: several primary sites (mozillafoundation.org, en.wikipedia.org, research.contrary.com) could not be fetched from this research environment due to network restrictions; their content is reported only via search snippets and secondary coverage.

## Q1. Per-app profile: anxiety offering, format, personalization, AI, pricing, scale, reviews, controversies

### Takeaway
The English-language market is dominated by a few well-funded, subscription-based players (Hallow, Pray.com, Abide, Glorify, Bible Chat) plus the free giant YouVersion; prices cluster at roughly $40-70/year. Anxiety is addressed mainly through audio meditations, sleep content and topical reading plans, not through clinical or symptom-based tools. Russian-language/Orthodox offerings are almost entirely static prayer books (молитвословы) with no anxiety-specific UX.

### Inferences
- Price anchor for the English market is ~$40/yr (Abide, Dwell low tier) to ~$70/yr (Hallow, Glorify); paywall resentment is the most common complaint category across Hallow, Glorify and Abide, suggesting room for a generous free tier (YouVersion and Lectio 365 show free models can achieve huge scale).
- Russian-language Orthodox apps are low-polish, low-review utilities (single-digit to hundreds of ratings), i.e., no well-funded incumbent occupies "Orthodox + anxiety" in RuStore.
- AI chat is the fastest-growing monetization model (Bible Chat ~$15M ARR early 2025) but carries theological-bias and privacy reputational risk, especially for Orthodox audiences whose hierarchs publicly warn against it.

## Q2. What is missing in the market (gaps)

### Takeaway
No surveyed app combines (a) symptom-based navigation ("I'm panicking now"), (b) clinical safety/crisis routing, (c) evidence-based CBT techniques integrated with Scripture, and (d) Russian-language Orthodox-compatible content. Each of these is either absent or delivered by separate apps.

### Cited Findings
- Faith apps are recommended alongside, not instead of, secular CBT apps (MindShift, Clear Fear) — evidence that CBT and Scripture are not integrated in one product — [Creative Family Counseling](https://creative-family-counseling.com/mental-health-and-wellness-apps)
- Reviewers note Abide is not a substitute for therapy for clinical anxiety and its search/discovery is weak — [learnofchrist.com](https://learnofchrist.com/resources/abide)
- Search found no evidence of faith-based apps integrating crisis features (988 etc.); only generic "call or text 988" lines on provider websites — [faith.tools](https://faith.tools/mental-health); [Banyan](https://faith.banyantreatmentcenter.com/mental-health/anxiety-and-addiction/)
- Hallow's content is Catholic-specific; Protestants prefer others; AI Bible bots skew US-evangelical — denominational neutrality is weak across the market — [learnofchrist comparison](https://learnofchrist.com/resources/compare/hallow-vs-pray-com-vs-abide); [Christian Today](https://www.christiantoday.com/news/concerns-raised-over-theological-bias-in-ai-bible-chatbots)
- Orthodox Russian apps are static prayer collections; only one Orthodox psychology app, with <1K downloads — [RuStore](https://www.rustore.ru/catalog/app/ru.orthopsy.app)
- Offline: Abide offers offline downloads (paid); «Православный Помощник» works offline; «Молитвослов» requires internet — [learnofchrist.com](https://learnofchrist.com/resources/abide); [RuStore](https://www.rustore.ru/catalog/app/com.yarolegovich.orthodoxhelper); [RuStore](https://www.rustore.ru/catalog/app/ru.orex.molitvoslov)
- Orthodox clergy guidance (Azbyka) already models "pray + see a psychologist" — a ready-made pastoral framing for a safety-routing feature — [Азбука](https://azbyka.ru/vopros/kakie-molitvy-pomogut-pri-trevoge/)
- Privacy distrust is a documented category issue (Mozilla 2022; Pray.com leak) — [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/mozilla-privacy-survey-finds-mental-health-and-prayer-apps-fail-privacy-test-pretty-spectacularly/)

### Inferences
- Strongest white space: a bilingual (RU/EN) app with symptom/feeling-first entry ("тревога перед будущим", "паника", "бессонница"), short verse + prayer + grounding/CBT micro-exercise, built-in crisis routing per country (e.g., 988 US; Russian helplines), offline by default, and privacy-first (no ad SDKs, local storage) as an explicit differentiator.
- Denominational neutrality can be handled by a "tradition" setting (Orthodox / Catholic / Protestant) controlling translations (Синодальный, KJV/NIV/ESV), prayer sets and calendar.
- Avoiding generative "AI pastor" chat (or tightly constraining it to retrieval of verses) could itself be a trust selling point for Orthodox users.

### Gaps
- No independent clinical studies found evaluating any Christian anxiety app's efficacy or safety.
- Did not verify whether Hallow/Abide/YouVersion show crisis resources in-app (absence in search ≠ absence in product); needs hands-on audit.

## Q3. Market size data for faith-based apps

### Takeaway
Hard data is thin; market-research reports put "spiritual wellness apps" at ~$1.9-2.8B (2025-26), with faith-based apps a minority share, while bottom-up App Store estimates suggest Bible/prayer apps earn on the order of $6.4M/month on iOS, heavily concentrated in the top 10.

### Cited Findings
- Global spiritual wellness apps market: ~$2.8B (2026) projected to $7.4B by 2033 (Research and Markets, Nov 2026) — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/02/3393420/28124/en/global-spiritual-wellness-apps-market-size-share-and-trends-report-2026-2033-digital-wellness-and-mindfulness-demand-propel-market-to-usd-7-4-billion.html)
- Other estimates: $1.85B (2025) and $2.56B (2025); one report assigns ~10% to religious/faith-based apps, 45% to meditation/mindfulness; 14.66% CAGR to 2035 — [Towards Healthcare](https://www.towardshealthcare.com/insights/spiritual-wellness-apps-market-sizing); [The Business Research Company](https://www.thebusinessresearchcompany.com/report/spiritual-wellness-apps-global-market-report); [Emergen Research](https://www.emergenresearch.com/industry-report/spiritual-wellness-apps-market)
- Bottom-up: analysis of 3,965 Bible & Prayer iOS apps estimated $6.4M/month combined; top 10 capture 76% of revenue (Dec 2025, unverified X post) — [Siro on X](https://x.com/Siron93/status/1995489375711265246)
- Bible Chat alone ~$15M ARR (Feb 2025); ~$750K net revenue in March 2025 — [Romania Insider](https://www.romania-insider.com/bible-chat-investment-round-faith-app-romania-feb-2025); [Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20250418/5-this-is-how-to-drive-demand-for-the-oldest-book-in-the-world)

### Inferences
- iOS-only bottom-up (~$77M/yr) is far below top-down "spiritual wellness" figures; top-down reports use broad definitions and should be treated as directional only.
- High concentration means a newcomer should target an underserved niche (Russian-language/Orthodox, clinical-safety-first) rather than compete head-on with Hallow/Abide in English.

### Gaps
- No Sensor Tower/Appfigures category report found; no market data at all for Russian-language Christian apps or RuStore revenue.
